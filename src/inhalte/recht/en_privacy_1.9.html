Privacy Policy for Tarot Cup

Version 1.9 · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This translation is for information only. Only the German version is legally binding.

This Privacy Policy applies to the app “Tarot Cup” and its website at https://tarot-cup.com. For the app, it applies in all versions, including test versions. On Google Play, the app has the package name at.tonc.kaffeesatztarot.

1. Key points at a glance

The app operates without an account and without advertising. It does not sell any data. Almost everything also works without internet access.

The regular readings are created on your phone from ready-made texts. Only the automatic reading is created on a server: once you have given your express consent and tap “Get a reading”, your dream, or your cards together with your question, are sent for this one reading to the Tarot Cup server in Germany and to Anthropic, where a language model writes the reading automatically. My server stores neither your text nor the reading, unless you report a reading (Section 10). Anthropic usually deletes them after 30 days at the latest and does not use them for training.

For the gifts for sharing and inviting, every link you share from the app carries a random code. My server knows your phone only through a random identifier, which it stores only as a non-reversible check value. It does not know your name, email address, or contacts. You delete your data on the server in the app’s Settings.

The app stores your history, notes, and settings on your phone. A copy is stored only by your phone’s backup in your Google account, if you have turned it on.

The app sends usage statistics and crash reports via Google Firebase only if you turn them on. Your readings, texts, and notes are never included.

The app asks for a question only for the automatic reading, and only if you want to enter one.

The website loads Google Analytics only if you agree. Without your consent, it stores only your choice in a necessary cookie. When you visit it, the host GitHub stores your IP address to protect the service.

You delete what the app has stored in the app itself or by uninstalling it. If you have questions, write me an email. You can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde).

Part A: The app

4. What the app stores on your phone

The app stores everything generated in this process on your phone. I do not have access to it.

History. For each reading, the app stores: the date and time, the type of reading (for example Card of the Day or coffee grounds), the cards drawn and whether they were reversed, for coffee grounds readings the chosen symbols with their place in the cup and the chosen area of life, for dream readings the chosen feeling, the chosen area of life and the chosen symbols, and the title of the reading. The app reassembles the text from this when you open the reading. An automatic reading goes into your history only if you tap “Save”. The app then stores the type (dream or cards), for cards the spread and the cards drawn, the character that gave the reading, and the text of the reading, but never your dream and never your question.

Notes. For each reading, you can write your own note, for example “What came of it?”. The note stays on your phone; if backup via Google is enabled, a copy may be stored in your Google account (Section 7). It does not appear in the image you share (Section 6).

Rhythm of the readings. Each reading has its rhythm, for example Three Cards every three days. For this, the app remembers when you last did which reading, and the Card of the Day for the day, so that it stays the same all day.

Gifts. For the gifts in the app, the app remembers: on which days you last drew the Card of the Day (only the last 7 days count); whether a gift for the week or for sharing is waiting; on which day you last received a gift for sharing; when you opened the New Year gift, until when all waiting times are therefore lifted, and that this entitles you to 30 days without ads, which only begin once there are ads in the app. For the New Year gift, the app reads the installation date from your phone’s system; it does not store it. These details stay on your phone: they are not sent and are not included in the backup (Section 7). If you have agreed to usage statistics, the only thing recorded is that a gift was created, redeemed, or opened, with the details from Section 9. The app does not remember which app you chose when sharing (Section 6).

Automatic reading, share streak, and inviting. For these, the app remembers: the random identifier of this installation for my server; your own code for links and invitations; whether and when you consented to the automatic reading, to which version of the consent text, and to which version of this Privacy Policy; how many automatic readings you have been given as gifts and still have open, and until when they are valid; whether you have already received an automatic reading (so that the welcome gift is given only once); from which day the next automatic reading is available; on which of the last 7 days you shared something, and whether one of your links was opened during that time; the numbers for the share streak and whether there is a welcome gift, as my server sets them, and whether the app has already looked them up; whether you came via an invitation, the code of this invitation, whether the invitation may be reported, its status, and on which day the app last reported a day for it (Section 10). The app stores these details only on your phone; they are not included in the backup (Section 7). The app does not store your dream or your question, except while you are typing it and the reading is in progress.

Character. For the illustrated character that keeps you company in the app (Aylin or Ozan), the app remembers: which of the two you chose or, if you did not choose one (for example with “Later”), which one it assigned to you once at random; whether the character’s tips are turned on in Settings; how often each walkthrough has already been shown to you, so that after the first few times it appears only briefly; how often the character has already appeared in large form in each place, and whether it has already introduced itself after your choice, so that it stays small afterwards; and on which day it last greeted you, so that this happens at most once a day. The app stores these details only on your phone; they are not included in the backup (Section 7). If you have agreed to usage statistics, the only things recorded are which character keeps you company, where and how you got it, and how you respond to its tips, with the details from Section 9; how often a walkthrough was shown or the character appeared in large form, and the day of the greeting, are not sent.

Settings. The app remembers: the language; whether, and at what time, it should remind you about the Card of the Day; that you confirmed at the first start that you are at least 16 years old; which version of the Terms of Use you accepted and when; when a new version was first shown to you; how much of the 30-day period has already passed and when the app last counted this; how many full readings you have completed and on which day it last asked you for a rating (Section 8); which version of this Privacy Policy you last opened in the app (so that it can mark a new version once, Section 20); how often you have already shuffled, so that the hint about shuffling appears only the first few times; whether you turned on usage statistics, and for which major version of this Privacy Policy (for version 1.9, this is 1), so that it asks you again only when there is a new major version (Section 9); on which day it first showed you the dream reading, so that the “New” label appears for only 14 days; whether it has already shown you the “New in Tarot Cup” window after an update, so that it appears only once; and whether the first start has been completed.

The app remembers all of this only on your phone. What the operating system may back up is set out in Section 7, and how you delete it in Section 15.

6. Sharing as an image

You can share a reading as a designed image, including an automatic reading. Before that, you see the image in a preview. It shows the cards drawn, either as pictures of the public-domain cards from 1909 or with their names only; for coffee grounds, the line drawings and names of the symbols; for dream readings, the names of the symbols. In addition, it shows the type of reading as a heading, the date, the name of the app, the address tarot-cup.com/app in small print, and, if you want, a sentence from the reading. Your notes, your dream, and your question are not on it. The image of an automatic reading says “created automatically” in small print, and the image file states in machine-readable form that the text was generated artificially (XMP field “Digital Source Type” according to IPTC). This field contains nothing about you.

The app passes the image to your phone’s share menu, together with a short text and a link to https://tarot-cup.com/app/. The link says whether a reading, the Card of the Day, a dream, or the app was shared, and carries your own code of 8 characters. My server assigns the code to your installation at random; the first time the app fetches it, the app sends your identifier to my server for this purpose (Section 10). The code reveals neither your name nor anything else about you, and anyone who receives the link cannot find out anything about you with it. When you share, the app briefly points out the code to you. The code is included by default; below, you can choose “share without code”. The link then goes out without a code and does not count toward the progress and the gift of the share streak; the app tells you this in a short sentence at that point. When you invite someone, there is no such choice, because there the code is the whole point. It serves only to let my server count whether one of your links was opened (share streak) and to make every share an invitation as well (Section 10). Without a connection to the server, the link stays without a code. If you choose “Ask a friend”, the link is preceded by “What do you think?”. Anyone who opens the link lands on the /app/ page of the website (Section 12). You decide which app you send the image and text to and to whom. The app you choose processes them under its own terms. Without this step, no reading leaves your phone, except in the backup, if you have turned it on (Section 7), and except for the automatic reading (Section 10).

When you share a reading, Android tells the app whether you chose an app in the share menu. The app does not read which app that was, does not store it, and does not measure it. It does not learn whether you actually send the message. The app uses this signal only for the gift for sharing (Section 4), for the share streak (Section 10), and, if you have agreed to usage statistics, to record that you closed the menu without choosing an app.

If you have agreed to usage statistics, the only things recorded are that you opened the preview, shared a reading or the app, shared an invitation, or closed the share menu without choosing an app, with the details from Section 9; the image, the text of the reading, your code, and the chosen app are not transmitted.

10. Automatic reading, gifts from the server, and inviting

For the automatic reading, the share streak, and inviting, there is a separate Tarot Cup server at api.tarot-cup.com. It is located in a data center of Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany. Hetzner operates it as my processor, on the basis of a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR. The app communicates with it only in encrypted form (HTTPS).

The automatic reading

For a dream, you can write down your dream in your own words; for the cards, you can ask the cards your own question. From this, a language model by Anthropic automatically writes a short reading. No human writes the reading. Normally, nobody reads your text; only if Anthropic’s safety systems flag a request can Anthropic review it (see below). The app shows the reading as a card marked “created automatically”.

You get the automatic reading as a gift: your first one as a welcome gift if you do not have and have not had any other gift for an automatic reading, otherwise through the share streak or through inviting (see below), at most one in 7 days per device; the welcome gift counts toward this limit. A gift expires 14 days after it was created; at most 2 can be open at a time. There is one welcome gift per device; it is only created when you request your first automatic reading, and therefore it does not expire. My server decides whether one is open and from when the next one is available.

Your consent

The first time you tap “Get a reading”, for a dream or for the cards, the app shows a window once. It says, the same for dreams and cards (version “e3” of the consent text): “Tapping “Agree and get a reading” gives your express consent for your dream or question, even if it involves health, faith or intimate matters, to be sent for your reading to our server in Germany and to our service provider outside the EU, for example in the US. There, a language model writes the reading automatically. Your text is used only for this and for security checks, never for training. This also applies if you report a reading. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings.” Below it, “More in the Privacy Policy” leads to this policy. The service provider is Anthropic: the contractual partner is Anthropic Ireland, and the computing takes place in the USA or in other countries outside the EU (see “Who writes the reading”). You choose “Agree and get a reading” or “Cancel”; both buttons are the same size. By choosing “Agree and get a reading”, you expressly consent to your details being processed as described in this section, including details about your health, your faith, your love and sex life, or other very personal matters. The app remembers on your phone that and when you consented, to which version of the consent text (currently “e3”), and to which version of this policy, and does not ask again after that. Without this consent, the app sends nothing to my server for the automatic reading. The consent is voluntary; all other features of the app also work without it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future in the app’s Settings under “Automatic reading”. After that, the app sends nothing more for the automatic reading until you agree again in the window. For a reading that has already been sent, an email to me is enough; by then, my server has already discarded your text, and at Anthropic it is deleted according to the periods below; a reported reading stays with me for up to 90 days without an identifier and cannot be attributed to you.

What is sent

If you have consented and tap “Get a reading”, the app sends to my server:

  • whether it is about a dream or about cards, and the length of the reading (currently only short),
  • for a dream, your text (at most 1,500 characters),
  • for cards, the spread, the cards drawn with their orientation and position and, if you enter one, your question (at most 300 characters),
  • the language of the app,
  • the country as a two-letter code, for example AT, so that a help text can name suitable places to turn to. The app takes it from the mobile network, otherwise from the SIM card, otherwise from your phone’s system settings; it does not need a location permission for this,
  • that it is a gifted reading and whether you have already received an automatic reading (only yes or no, for the welcome gift),
  • the random identifier of this installation and the verification from Google Play (see “Protection against misuse”).

Please do not write other people’s names, addresses, or phone numbers in your text; they are not needed for the reading.

Who writes the reading

My server passes your text, or your cards and your question, together with the language and the country to Anthropic’s Claude API. There, Anthropic writes the reading with a language model and sends it back to my server, which checks it and passes it to the app. If a reading does not pass the check, my server can request it a second time or send a fixed substitute text. If the language model declines a request for safety reasons, Anthropic can answer it with another of its own language models; this stays with the same provider and under the same terms. Your identifier, your code, and your IP address are not sent to Anthropic.

Anthropic processes the data as my processor, on my instructions, and only to create the reading. The basis is Anthropic’s data processing agreement, which is part of Anthropic’s commercial terms. My contractual partner is Anthropic Ireland, Limited, 6th Floor, South Bank House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, D04 TR29, Ireland. The processing is also carried out by the parent company Anthropic, PBC, 548 Market St, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA, with further sub-processors. Anthropic determines where exactly the computing takes place; this may be in the USA or in other countries outside the EU. The basis for this transfer is the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses in the agreement with Anthropic (Section 14).

Anthropic keeps your inputs and the readings for up to 30 days and does not use them to train language models; according to Anthropic, it deletes API inputs and outputs no later than 30 days after they are received or generated. Anthropic may keep them longer only if the law requires it or if Anthropic’s automated safety systems flag a request as a possible violation of Anthropic’s usage policy: in that case, the request and the response for up to 2 years, and the assessment by these systems for up to 7 years. More on this: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data

Help instead of a reading

Before your text goes to Anthropic, my server checks it against lists of words to see whether it sounds like a crisis or danger in your real life, for example thoughts of self-harm or violence that is really happening to you. A dream about death or violence on its own gets a normal reading. If this check is triggered, your text does not go to Anthropic. Instead, you get a fixed help text. For Austria, Germany, Switzerland, France, the United Kingdom, the USA, Mexico, Brazil, India, Indonesia, and Hong Kong, it names a place that helps in that country; otherwise, it asks you to contact the emergency number in your country or a person you trust. If it is only the language model that recognizes such a situation, my server discards its response and likewise sends only the fixed help text. This check is part of the reading you consented to. My server does not store to whom a help text was shown; it counts, without an identifier, how often this happened per day, and its operating log records the type of response without an identifier (see “Limits and numbers”).

What is stored

My server does not store your dream, your question, or the reading; it stores only a reading that you report (see “Reporting”). It holds them only in memory while the request is running and discards them afterwards. Nor does it store your IP address, which, like any server, it needs in order to respond, or the result of the check by Google Play. It keeps no log of access. All that is stored per device is what is needed for the limits and gifts (see “Protection against misuse” and Section 15). The app shows the reading and forgets it, unless you tap “Save” (Section 4).

Reporting

Below an automatic reading, right after it has appeared, there is the “Report” button. If you find a reading hurtful, inappropriate, or wrong, you can use it to report the reading to me without leaving the app. Only the text of the reading, whether it was a reading, a notice, or a substitute text, the language, and the country are then sent, plus, as with every request, your identifier and the verification from Google Play. Only the text of the reading, the type, the language, the country, and the date are stored, without an identifier; my server deletes them automatically after 90 days. The basis is your consent to the automatic reading, which also covers reported readings. Only for the limit of 10 reports per day does my server count per device how often you have reported on that day. Your dream and your question are never reported. I read reported readings to find and fix errors.

Gifts from the server: the share streak

The gift for sharing remains a spread (Section 4). In addition, there is the share streak; it counts only links with a code, not those you share “without code” (Section 6): if you share something from the app on 3 different days within 7 days and at least one of your links was opened during these 7 days, you also get an automatic reading. The app counts the days on which you shared only on your phone. After you share and when you open your gifts, it asks my server whether one of your links was opened. Once the days are reached, it fetches the gift and sends the number of days for this. For this, my server remembers per device the day of the last gift from the streak. It learns whether a link was opened via the /app/ page of the website (Section 12). In the app, you only see that one of your links was opened, not by whom.

The app asks my server at startup for the numbers of the streak (how many days, how long, how many opened links) and whether there is currently a welcome gift, but only if you already use the automatic reading, sharing with a code, or inviting; otherwise, fixed numbers in the app apply. This request contains no identifier and no details about you.

Inviting

On the page with your gifts, you can invite someone. For this, the app fetches your code from my server (the same code as in your shared links) and passes a link to the share menu. You decide whom you send it to; the app does not read any contacts.

If the invited person opens the link on an Android phone, the /app/ page passes the code on to Google Play (Section 12). On the screen at the first start, there is a line for everyone saying that, with an invitation, the days of use are reported without names and that this can be turned off in Settings. Only after the person has accepted the Terms of Use with one of the two buttons does the app read the referral information from Google Play, and from it only the invitation code (Section 8). If it finds one, the invitation counts as accepted. The app then sends the code to my server and afterwards reports once on each day on which it shows a reading that the app was used on that day, even if the reading was created without the internet, provided the phone still has a connection on that day. Only this day is reported, no reading, no name, no email address, and no contacts. The invited person can turn this off at any time in Settings under “Send invitation days”; the app then no longer reports any days.

If the app has been used on 3 different days within 30 days (counted by the server’s clock), both get an automatic reading as a gift; the person who invites gets at most 3 per month. The person who invites only sees how many invitations have already brought a gift that month, not who was invited. If someone has invited only one person, they can conclude from this that this person used the app on 3 days.

For inviting and the gifts, my server stores: per device, the check value of the identifier, the code, the first and last contact, and whether the device was invited; per invitation, the check value of the invited person’s identifier, the inviting person’s code, the date, and the reported days; per gift, the day on which it was created and where it comes from (welcome, sharing, inviting, or invitation accepted); and per device, how many invitations were rewarded per month. The periods are set out in Section 15.

If you use the share streak, inviting, or the automatic reading, the app asks my server at startup whether any gifts are open for you.

Protection against misuse

Identifier. When it is first needed, the app generates a random identifier of 32 characters for this installation and sends it to my server with every request, except when it asks for the numbers of the streak. My server does not store the identifier in plain text, but only as a non-reversible check value (HMAC-SHA-256 with a secret key that is kept only on the server). This lets it recognize your device in order to apply gifts, the limit of one automatic reading in 7 days, and daily limits, but it does not know who you are.

Verification from Google Play (Play Integrity). So that only the genuine app from Google Play on a genuine device uses the server and nobody obtains gifts or readings by deception, the app obtains a verification from Google Play, the Play Integrity API, before every request to my server (except the request for the numbers). In doing so, it gives Google only a check value of the request, not its content. According to Google, Google processes for this purpose details about the app (package name, version, signature), whether the app was obtained via Google Play for the Google accounts on the phone, and details on the authenticity of the device that Google Play generates. According to Google, this data is encrypted, not passed on to third parties, and deleted after a fixed period. My server has Google decrypt the verification, decides on that basis whether and how many requests it accepts, and does not store the result. If the verification is missing, the app says that the automatic reading is not working right now. More on this: https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/terms

Limits and numbers. My server counts the requests per device and day so that nobody overloads it. It also keeps daily totals without an identifier, for example how many readings of which type were created, how many help texts and substitute texts, how many characters the language model used, and what that cost. Its operating log contains only the type, the language, the country, such numbers, and errors, no IP address, no identifier, no text, and no verification.

Deleting your data on the server

In the app’s Settings, “Delete my data on the server” immediately deletes everything my server has stored about your device: device and code, invitation, open gifts, counters, rewards, the date of the last gift from the streak, and the counted openings of your links. Open gifts from the server are lost in the process. The app then generates a new identifier; it keeps the day from which the next automatic reading is available, and whether you have already received an automatic reading, on your phone. If you have already received one, you therefore do not get a new welcome gift afterward either. Reported readings and the daily totals carry no identifier and therefore cannot be assigned to any device. Deleted details may remain in the server’s backups for up to 14 days: in the nightly copy of the database (7 slots, one per weekday) and in the backup at Hetzner (7 slots), until these are overwritten.
