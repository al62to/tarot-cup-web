3. What the app is and what it is not

The app accompanies you through old rituals: reading coffee grounds, tarot, and dreams. For coffee grounds, you choose the symbols you see in your cup yourself. For tarot, you draw cards. From this, the app puts together a text on your phone according to fixed rules, which follows the traditional meanings of the symbols and cards.

Automatic reading. With the automatic reading, you write down your dream in your own words or ask the cards your own question. From this, a language model by Anthropic automatically writes a text, without a human reading it first. Such a text may repeat itself, be inappropriate, claim things that are not true, or misunderstand your question. Therefore, do not rely on statements in it without checking them yourself. If your text sounds as if you are in a crisis or in danger in real life, you get a notice of where to find help instead of a reading. This notice does not replace an emergency call: if you or someone else is in danger, contact your country’s emergency number. I may refuse an automatic reading, shorten it, or replace it with a fixed text, for example if it does not pass my checks.

Reporting. If you find an automatic reading hurtful, inappropriate, or wrong, you can report it in the app. I look at reports, but I do not have to reply to you or change a reading.

This is the whole service of the app: entertaining texts about symbols, cards, and dreams and the features you see in the app, such as the Dictionary, your history, your notes, the gifts, and the automatic reading. The app does not predict anything true or certain about your past, present, or future, and does not promise to. That a reading does not come true or does not fit your life is therefore not a defect of the app.

Please also note:

  • No advice. The app does not replace medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice. Even if a reading touches on topics such as health, money, law, or relationships, it says nothing about how things really stand for you or other people.
  • Your decisions are yours to make, on your own responsibility. Do not base important decisions on a reading. With important questions, talk to people you trust or to professionals.
  • Your question. For the regular spreads, the app does not ask for your question. You only think of it, as tradition has it. You write down your own question or your dream only for the automatic reading.
