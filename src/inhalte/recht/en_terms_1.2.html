From this, the app puts together a text on your phone according to fixed rules, which follows the traditional meanings of the symbols and cards.

This is the whole service of the app: entertaining texts about symbols and cards and the features you see in the app, such as the Dictionary, your history, and your notes. The app does not predict anything true or certain about your past, present, or future, and does not promise to. That a reading does not come true or does not fit your life is therefore not a defect of the app.
